# Flambeur v2: the fun rework

Decided with Evan on Nov 18 2026, over 7 rounds on the ideation board. This is the build spec. Where it disagrees with DESIGN.md, this wins, and DESIGN.md gets rewritten slice by slice as each part lands (the usual "rule changes update DESIGN.md" rule still applies). Anything marked *(tune)* is a first-pass number, and the sim sets the real one.

## Why

Evan playtested it and it wasn't fun. The sim backs up all four reasons:

1. **Not enough battle.** A round was one poker hand: lock once, bet once or twice. The bot spent 84% of its actions in the shop.
2. **Deckbuilding didn't feel needed.** It is needed: a bot that never buys wins 2.3% vs Medium, and one that buys wins 45%. But you never see why you lost, so a loss reads as "they drew a flush," not "my deck is weak."
3. **No visible, rising threat.** The rival's hands grow ~7× over a run, and you only see them at showdown. Balatro's target grows ~300× and is on screen the whole time.
4. **No hook.** Upgrades mostly add instead of multiply, so runs never break open, and there's nothing to come back for.

Also: only 6% of sim runs reach round 12. Half are over by round 7, mostly from busts at forced boss rounds.

4. Sim, Classic, greedy vs Medium, 1000 runs:

## 1. Words

- **Hand**: one of the up to 3 plays in a round. The UI says "Hand 2 of 3." The 8 cards you hold are "your cards." Balatro uses "hand" both ways too.
- **Round**: one duel against one opponent, best of 3 hands.
- **Table**: 3 rounds against the same rival. Round 3 of each Table is the boss.
- **The Sleeve**: your 2 insert slots (§9).

## 2. The run

- Pick a starter box (3 offered, seeded). Each box is a full 2–A deck. It's dealt into **4 rows of 6, and you keep 3 rows = 18 cards** (§13).
- **4 Tables × 3 rounds = 12 rounds.** Each Table, a new random rival sits down (§11). Rounds 3, 6, 9, 12 are bosses: one twist (§15) and a bigger payout.
- You start with **$20** *(tune)*, and you owe **Le Patron** (§10).
- **Win:** pay Le Patron's final payment after round 12. **Lose:** go bust, or miss a payment.
- **Score** = your bankroll after the final payment × difficulty (Easy 1, Medium 1.5, High Roller 2). Leaderboards stay per seed per difficulty.
- **Difficulty** only changes the rival bots (how sharp they bet, how fast they grow). It never changes the rules.

## 3. A round

1. **Deal 8.** The whole deck is shuffled at the start of every round (stream `seed:round:pile`), so Tables no longer hold a pile.
2. **Hand 1.** Before you lock you can:
   - **Discard**: toss 1–5 cards and draw that many. **3 discards per round** in total (Classic: 4).
   - **Use inserts** from the Sleeve on your cards (§9).
   Then lock 1–5 cards as **Play** or **Flamber** (§5). Both seats lock at once, and neither sees the other's.
3. **The opening bet** (§4). The ante goes in, then Min or Max.
4. **Reveal hand 1.** Both appraisals count up side by side (~2 s, skippable). The higher one takes the hand. A tie goes to nobody.
5. **Refill** back to 8 from the pile. If the pile runs out, you play with what you have.
6. **Hand 2**: discard, inserts, lock, reveal.
7. If someone has 2 hands, or a knockout happened (§6), the round is over.
8. **Hand 3**: lock, then **the last push** (§4), then reveal.
9. **Who wins the round:** more hands won. Tied on hands → higher total appraisal across the round's hands (thrown hands count 0). Still tied → a tie round: bets come back, nobody gets the House bonus.
10. **Payout** and the round-end tally (§12).
11. **Spoils** (§7).
12. **Cleanup:** everything goes back into the deck (played, discarded, unplayed, plus new ★ cards and Bulk). Then a shop (§8), unless the run is over.

A hand you can't play because you have no cards counts as a thrown hand.

Rival sees: your card count, discards used, and how many inserts are in your Sleeve. Never what you locked, or which inserts. Marked knockoffs still show face up.

## 4. Betting

> **Nov 20:** a seat that flambéd the hand it just locked isn't asked to bet: it stays where it is and shows as a Min. Rules in DESIGN.md, Betting; why in docs/FLAMBER.md §1b.

No fold, anywhere. The rule on the button:

> **Max: bet $X more on yourself. Win and the House pays it, lose and your rival takes it.**

- **Ante:** both seats pay the round's Min into the pot. The round's winner takes the pot.
- **Opening bet** (after locking hand 1): each seat picks **Min** or **Max**. The higher bankroll picks first, the lower one second after seeing it (tie: alternate by round, like today). Max puts the difference (Max − Min) on yourself:
  - Win the round: the House pays you that amount.
  - Lose the round: it goes to your rival.
  - Tie round: it comes back.
- **Both on Max** is the big payout: the winner gets the House's payment for their own Max **and** the loser's Max.
- **The last push** (after locking hand 3, only when there is a hand 3): anyone still on Min may switch to Max. Same order.
- You can only pick Max if you can cover it. Under the ante you're all-in for what you have, like today.
- Underdog perks: acting second is the only one left. Peek is cut.

## 5. Flamber

> **Nov 20:** a flambé doesn't bet, and on screen it burns at the lock, not the reveal. See DESIGN.md, Flamber and docs/FLAMBER.md.

Flamber moves out of the betting buttons and onto the action bar next to Play and Discard. One line:

> **Flamber: throw this hand. A pair or better merges into a ★ card. Otherwise, one card you pick gets +1 Value.**

- **Pair or better:** the locked cards merge exactly like today (`forgePlan` / `buildForged`: traits, tiers, trait slots, Catalyst). The new ★ card goes **straight into your cards** for the next hand, not into the pile, and stays in your deck for good. Every card eaten beyond the first leaves a Bulk card, which goes to this round's discard and joins the deck at cleanup.
- **No pair:** you pick one locked card (if you locked one card, it's that one). It comes back to your cards with **+1 Value forever** (the old fold grind, so Participation Trophy still works). The other locked cards go to the discard.
- **The hand is thrown:** it scores 0, it loses to any played hand, it ties another thrown hand, and it **can't be knocked out**.
- No money cost. The price is one of your three hands.
- Over the trait cap, the trait pick (`forgePick`) works like today.

The plays it opens up: throw hand 1 to forge a bomb for hands 2 and 3, or throw hand 3 to lose this round but carry the card into every round after.

## 6. Knockout

Win a hand by **K× or more** and the round ends right there, a win for you.

| Table | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | Sudden death |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K *(tuned)* | 6× | 8× | 10× | 12× | 12× |

- The HUD shows it ("K.O. 6×"). The first pass was 2/3/5/8; the sim moved it to 6/8/10/12 (DESIGN.md, Tuning).
- A thrown hand can't be knocked out.
- Lowball twist: you knock out by being K× **lower**.
- A knockout pays a bonus (§12).

## 7. Spoils

Spoils replace the free pack.

- After every round (not after the last one), **3 cards go on the table**, from stream `seed:round:spoils:<pair>`, so both seats see the same 3.
- **The loser picks first, then the winner, and the last card burns.** In a tie round, the lower bankroll picks first.
- Either seat can **skip for +$1** *(tune)*. The next picker chooses from whatever's left.
- Cards: plain 2–A of random suits, with a 10% chance of a finish, 5% a stamp, 3% a card type *(tune)*. No knockoffs or Jokers, which stay in packs.
- A picked card goes into your deck.
- It's random on purpose (Evan): no "fits your deck" weighting, so the seed stays fair.

## 8. Shops

A small shop after most rounds, and a big one between Tables. The free pack is gone.

| | Small shop (after rounds 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11) | Big shop (after rounds 3, 6, 9) |
|---|---|---|
| At the door | | Le Patron collects (§10), and the scouting report for the next rival |
| Packs | 2, random from Off-Brand / Repack / Collector | 3 |
| Collectibles | 1 | 3, one always a ×Hype payoff (§14) |
| Register | 1 scratcher, $3 | 2 scratchers |
| Service | one: the grading desk at a Table's 1st small shop, the Bulk bin at its 2nd | the grading desk and the Bulk bin |
| Pochette (the old Sleeves, §21) | | one offered, $10 |
| Always | restock ($1, $2… per shop), sell from the binder (STONKS) | same |
| Multiplayer timer | 20 s | 45 s |

- Restock rerolls packs, collectibles and scratchers, never the pochette.
- The grading desk works like today: one card per shop, back at the next shop. Freshness still counts in rounds.
- The shopkeeper sign, the no-repeats-until-the-pool-is-shown rule, knockoffs, Repacks, STONKS and pack lanes all stay as they are.
- No shop before round 1.

## 9. The Sleeve and inserts

Your **Sleeve** holds **2 inserts**, drawn as 2 face-down cards beside your hand (cards up your sleeve). It replaces scratchers resolving on the spot.

- Scratch a ticket and its insert goes into the Sleeve. If it's full, use one or toss one (the new one or an old one).
- **Grail** still resolves on the spot and never takes a slot.
- **In-hand** inserts are used during a round, before a lock, on the cards you're holding, like Balatro's tarots. That's where the strategy is: fix the suit right when it finishes your flush.

| Insert | When | Does | Odds *(tune)* |
|---|---|---|---|
| Code | anytime | Level up a hand type (+15 Value, +1 Hype) | 18 |
| Checklist | in hand | Up to 2 cards become a suit you pick | 11 |
| Stylo | in hand | Up to 2 cards go up one rank (an Ace stays an Ace) | 11 |
| Broyeuse | in hand | Shred up to 2 cards for good | 9 |
| Photocopie | in hand | Select 2: the left one becomes a copy of the right (rank, suit, finish, stamp). Not Jokers or Bulk | 8 |
| Reprint | in hand | A copy of 1 card goes into your deck (this round's discard) | 7 |
| Foil wrap | in hand | 1 card becomes Foil (replaces its finish) | 7 |
| Holo wrap | in hand | 1 card becomes Holo | 7 |
| Prism wrap | in hand | 1 card becomes Prism | 2 |
| Tampon | in hand | 1 card gets a random stamp: 1st Edition, Signed or Gold Border (replaces its stamp or knockoff) | 6 |
| Allumettes | in hand | Merge 2 same-rank cards in your hand into a ★ card now, like flambéing a Pair, without throwing a hand. Leaves 1 Bulk | 6 |
| Wishlist | in the shop | 1 binder card shuffles into your deck (the Joker cap still applies) | 5 |
| Grail | on the spot | A free rare collectible or a Joker | 3 |

Odds add up to 100. Wishlist with an empty binder becomes a Code, like today. Using an insert is one logged action, and the 20 s lock timer covers it.

## 10. Le Patron's marker

> **You owe Le Patron. Pay at the door of the next big shop, or you're out.**

| Due | After Table 1 | After Table 2 | After Table 3 | After round 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Payment *(tune)* | $6 | $15 | $30 | $60, settle up |

- Paid automatically when you walk into the big shop, before anything else. Can't cover it → you're out (counts as a bust).
- The HUD always shows the next payment: "Le Patron · $15 at the next big shop."
- **Solo:** only you owe it. Rivals are guests at your table.
- **Multiplayer:** every player owes the same schedule. In battle royale that's the cull: it trims whoever's doing worst, on a schedule everyone can see. Two players missing the same payment in a 1v1: the one with more money wins.
- To make room for it: start at $20, and nobody can be forced to Max (§4). Busts should come from the marker on a schedule you can see, not out of nowhere at a boss.

## 11. Rivals (solo)

- **All 10 masks** are rivals. A run draws 4 different ones, seeded (`seed:rivals`), so the daily is the same for everyone.
- Each rival **sits down with a stack**: $20 / $40 / $70 / $120 at Tables 1–4 *(tune)*.
- **Bust the rival** and you've taken its stack. The Table ends right there and you go straight to the big shop. Each skipped round pays a skip bonus of 2× its ante *(tune)*, so ending early never scores worse than stalling. The round counter jumps ahead (bust the Table 1 rival in round 2, and round 4 is next).
- Nobody busts by the end of the boss → the rival leaves with what's left.
- **Building them:** a solo rival is built when it sits down, to match its Table: an 18-card lane deck, plus 2 upgraded lane cards and 1 lane collectible for every Table before this one. Difficulty shifts that by a step (Easy one behind, High Roller one ahead plus a starting rare). It plays rounds and takes spoils like a player, but it doesn't shop and doesn't owe the marker.
- The scouting report in the big shop shows the next rival's mask, lane, case, deck size and the boss twist.

| Mask | Lane | Deck leans toward | Case, gained by Table (2 → 3 → 4) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hare | Pairs | 3 copies of 4 ranks. Nervous: bets Min unless it's sure | Rubber Band → Les Jumeaux → Binder Divider |
| Pierrot | Low cards | 2–6 | Low Ball → Petite Monnaie → Odd Lot |
| Black Domino | Straights | Runs of connected ranks | Straight Edge → Ligne Droite → Code: Straight +1 |
| Crimson Domino | Money | Gold Borders, bets Max a lot | Price Sticker → Le Flambeur → Tournament Badge |
| Arlequin | Finishes | Finishes and Stickers | Black Light → L'Écrin → Lucky Penny |
| Queen of Hearts | ♥ flushes | Hearts, Holo | Color-Sorted Binder → Sommelier → Playmat (♥) |
| King of Spades | Flambé | ★ cards | Chalumeau → Flamme Bleue → Chef's Apron |
| Owl | Grade | Graded cards, Prism | PSA 10 Slab → Le Commissaire-priseur → Thin Binder |
| Scaramouche | Junk | Bulk and knockoffs | Bulk Bin → Le Ferrailleur → Participation Trophy |
| Fox | Jokers | 2 Jokers, Signed cards | Specialist → Bis ! → Combo Platter |

The three old difficulty masks (Hare, Scaramouche, King of Spades) were fixed per difficulty. Now any mask can show up at any difficulty.

## 14. Collectibles and lanes

A **lane** is a build ("pairs deck"). Cheap collectibles add, and one rare payoff per lane multiplies. The case stays 5 slots.

**Order matters, like Balatro.** The case triggers left to right, and a × multiplies whatever Hype has built up so far. So a + to the left of a × gets multiplied, and a + to the right doesn't. On the case: "Left to right."

Scoring order: hand base → each scored card in play order (Value, finish, stamps, tier, grade, then each collectible's per-card effect, left to right) → Bulk → each collectible's per-hand effect, left to right. Hype can be fractional now: show one decimal, and round the total down at the end.

The 10 payoffs (all rare, $9):

| Payoff | Lane | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Les Jumeaux | Pairs | Each Pair in the hand: ×1.5 Hype (Pair 1, Two Pair 2, Full House 1, bonus hands count) |
| Sommelier | Flush | Flush: ×2 Hype if a scored card is Holo |
| Flamme Bleue | Flambé | Each scored flambéed card: ×1.5 Hype |
| L'Écrin (was Vitrine) | Finishes | ×1 Hype per different Finish scored (min ×1; Stickers count as the finish they wear) |
| Petite Monnaie | Low cards | Each scored 2–6: ×1.25 Hype |
| Le Ferrailleur | Bulk | Each Bulk played: ×1.5 Hype |
| Le Flambeur | Money | ×Hype = 1 + 1 per $20 you hold *(tune, might be too strong)* |
| Bis ! | Retrigger | Signed cards score 3 times, not 2 |
| Ligne Droite | Straights | Straight (or a straight bonus hand): ×2.5 Hype |
| Le Commissaire-priseur | Grade | Each scored graded card: ×1.5 Hype |

New feeder: **Bulk Bin** (common): each Bulk played +2 Hype. The other feeders already exist (the rival table in §11 lists them by lane). Leftovers (Lucky Penny, Top Loader, Deck Box, Team Bag, Thin Binder…) stay as filler. Cut whatever the sim shows nobody picks.

Payoff odds: they're rare (8% weight), and the big shop guarantees one, so you see at least 3 a run.

## 15. Things that break, and the rewrites

| Rule | Why it breaks | Rewrite |
|---|---|---|
| Bluff (Joker) | "+$5 if the rival folds," and nobody folds | **Poker Face:** played in a round you lose, your Max bet comes back |
| Sticky Fingers | It was per showdown | Win the round with it played in any hand: steal a random card from the rival's deck. Once per round |
| Double or Nothing | The pot works differently | Everything this round pays or costs ×2 (played in any hand by either side, as far as the loser's bankroll goes) |
| Rival's Card | It was per showdown | At each reveal, it copies the rival's highest played card in that hand |
| Gold Border | It was per showdown | Once per round: played in any hand, +$3 if you win the round, −$3 if you lose it |
| Cold Deck (twist) | No mulligans | No discards this round |
| High Stakes (twist) | Min = Max | Everyone is on Max this round |
| Lowball (twist) | | Lowest takes each hand, and KO is inverted (§6) |
| Other twists | | Apply to all three hands |
| Classic box | "First mulligan free" | 4 discards |
| High Roller box | It started at $30 against $15 | Start +$10 |
| Momentum | It counted showdowns won | Counts rounds won this Table |
| Chip-lead win | The rival changes every Table | Pay off Le Patron (solo). 1v1: both paid off → the higher bankroll wins |

New boss twist, now that 2s exist: **Deuces Wild**, where 2s count as any rank *(optional, add if it plays well)*.

## 16. Multiplayer

Same rules as solo. **Live first**: the rooms from the online build. Async comes later and isn't in this spec, because you can't bluff a ghost.

- **1v1:** one opponent the whole game. Both owe Le Patron.
- **Battle royale (up to 10):** pairs are re-drawn (seeded) at the start of every Table, matching solo's new rival every Table. A bot fills an odd seat. Last one standing wins. Sudden death past 12 (the ante doubles each round). Seat bots are full players: they shop and owe the marker.
- **Early finishers** (a 2–0 or a KO) pick spoils first, then watch another table from the rail.
- **Timers:** lock 20 s per hand (covers discards and inserts), bet 10 s, spoils 10 s, small shop 20 s, big shop 45 s, count-up ~2 s and skippable.
- **Timeouts:** lock = the bot's best play, bet = Min, spoils = the bot's pick, shop = leave.

## 20. Bots, sim and tests

**Bots** (`bot.ts`) need: discards (chase the best hand the cards could make), when to throw a hand (flambé a pair when they're up or the pair is weak), Max on strength vs `REF_SCORE` and again at the last push, spoils picks (highest value to their deck, hate-draft a suit the opponent's locked cards show), insert use, and solo rivals growing in their lane. Add a `nobuy` style to `STYLES` for §0.

**Sim** (`sim/econ.ts`) prints, on top of today's table: decisions per round, hands per round, % of runs reaching round 12, KO %, shop share of your actions, marker misses by Table, flambés per round, inserts used per run.

**New tests**, with expected numbers worked out by hand:
- Best of 3: 2–0 ends after hand 2, 1–1 goes to hand 3, a tied round goes to the higher total, a fully tied round returns the bets.
- Knockout at each Table's K. A thrown hand can't be knocked out.
- Flamber on a Pair puts a ★ card in your cards for the next hand and 1 Bulk in the discard. Flamber with no pair gives the picked card +1 Value.
- The self-bet money: Min/Min, Max vs Min (win and lose), Max/Max, a tie round.
- The marker: a payment at the big shop door, and a bust when you can't cover it.
- Busting a rival skips the rest of its Table and pays the skip bonus.
- Spoils order: loser first, a tie goes to the lower bankroll, skip pays +$1, the last card burns.
- Scoring order: a + to the left of a × gets multiplied, and to the right it doesn't.
- Each in-hand insert on a known hand.
